MyTube

MyTube is a personal, non-commercial Android TV app for Google TV. It shows the videos of the YouTube channels you are subscribed to in a TV-friendly layout:

  • upcoming live streams and premieres,
  • channels that are live right now,
  • recent uploads grouped into categories you choose (no Shorts).

Selecting a video opens it in the official YouTube app. MyTube does not play, download or modify any video.

Google sign-in

MyTube uses Google sign-in with the read-only YouTube permission (youtube.readonly) only to read your list of subscriptions. It never posts, likes, subscribes or changes anything on your account.
