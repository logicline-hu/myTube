MyTube – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MyTube is a personal, non-commercial Android TV app. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, MyTube requests the read-only YouTube scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly). With it the app reads:

  • the list of YouTube channels you are subscribed to (channel name, ID and avatar),
  • public information about those channels' videos (title, thumbnail, duration, view count, category, live status, scheduled start time).

The app does not access your email address, contacts, watch history, comments, playlists or any other data, and it never makes changes to your YouTube or Google account.

How data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to display your subscription feed on your TV.
  • Everything is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage: the sign-in token, a cache of video information, and the categories you assign to channels.
  • MyTube has no servers of its own. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party; the app only communicates directly with Google/YouTube services.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MyTube uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing your data

  • Use Settings → Sign out in the app, or uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the support email address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
